# Discovery Question Bank

*Week 2. A discovery call finds the customer's real problem before you pitch anything. Aim to listen ~70% of the time, ask open questions, and summarise back before moving on. Add your own questions as you practise.*

## Current state
- Walk me through how this works today.
- What tools or systems are involved? Who built them, and who maintains them?
- What does the data flow look like end to end?

## Pain
- What's not working well right now?
- When did this become a priority? What changed?
- What have you already tried?

## Impact
- What does this problem cost you: time, money, customers, risk?
- Who feels it most?
- What happens if nothing changes in the next 6–12 months?

## Success criteria
- If this goes perfectly, what's different in 6 months?
- How would you measure success?
- What would make you say this project failed?

## Technical constraints
- Which systems would this need to integrate with?
- Any security, compliance or data residency requirements?
- What volumes are we talking about (requests, records, users)?
- What can your team change on your side, and what's off limits?

## Decision process
- Who else is involved in this decision?
- What does your evaluation process look like, and what's the timeline?
- Are you looking at other options, including building it yourselves?

## Summarise back (always end with this)
- "So what I'm hearing is ... Have I got that right? What did I miss?"
- Agree a concrete next step (a technical deep dive, a demo, a pilot).
