# Objection Handling

*Week 6. An objection is usually a question about risk. Don't argue with it: find the real concern, then answer that.*

## The pattern
1. **Acknowledge:** "That's a fair concern, and a lot of teams raise it."
2. **Clarify:** "Can you tell me more about what's behind that?" Find the real need.
3. **Respond:** answer the real concern with specifics, options and honest trade-offs.
4. **Confirm:** "Does that address it, or is there something else?"
5. **Know when to escalate:** pricing, legal and roadmap promises go to the right person.
